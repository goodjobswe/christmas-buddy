# Changelog

## 2.0.0

- A new village with automatic seasons and manual spring, summer, autumn and winter choices.
- Pip hides somewhere new every day, with clues and a December advent calendar.
- Christmas Eve or Christmas Day countdowns, growing decorations and seasonal surprises.
- Santa's gift lists, six carols, local December reminders and countdown sharing.
- Android screenshots, privacy information and accessible open-source licences.
- Dedicated Android release signing and recovery from damaged gift-list entries.

This is the first GitHub APK release of the redesigned app. It may require a fresh installation over development builds or the original Google Play edition because their signing keys differ.
