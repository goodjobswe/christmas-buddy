# Privacy

Christmas Buddy works offline. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and does not send your lists, settings or discoveries to goodjob or an app server.

Your gift lists, settings and Pip discoveries are stored in the app's private storage on your Android device. Android may back up or transfer that data according to your device and account settings. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling removes the local copy; a system backup may restore it later.

Daily December reminders are optional local notifications. Android asks for notification permission when needed. You can turn reminders off in Settings or revoke permission in Android settings.

Sharing opens Android's share sheet with a picture of your countdown and scene. You choose the receiving app and whether to send it. Website and source-code links open in your browser only when tapped; those services have their own privacy policies.

Questions or issues can be reported through the [project repository](https://github.com/goodjobswe/christmas-buddy) or [goodjob.nu](https://goodjob.nu).
